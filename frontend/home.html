Good to know

Is TheRepeat completely free to use?

Yes. TheRepeat is designed to be free to explore and listen to. The music is streamed through YouTube, so normal YouTube availability and viewing rules still apply, and ads may appear depending on the content and device.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No account is required. You can jump straight in, browse categories, pick a vibe, and start playing without any registration or login process.

Why is the music playing through YouTube?

TheRepeat uses YouTube’s streaming infrastructure to power its playlists. It lets us keep the experience fast and easy, while giving you access to a large library of music without building a separate music catalog from scratch.

Does the music start automatically?

No. For a smoother experience, the playlist loads in a paused state first. You can tap play whenever you’re ready, or choose a different category to switch the mood instantly.

Can I suggest a new category or playlist?

Absolutely. If there’s a vibe, route, or sound you want to see added, send us your suggestion through the feedback page. We’re always looking for more ways to match the moment.
